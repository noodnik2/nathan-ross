Saturday, July 31

My darling Anne—

The time is 5:30 A.M. and it’s getting light outside but I can no sooner sleep than fly. Have been tossing and turning
for the past 2½ hours—trying to banish things for turning over and over again in my mind but no use. I just lie
there thinking about us and I get angrier and lonier. Angrier with myself for being so darn weak and so easily
confused and influenced. Lonier because I miss you and need you so terribly—but I guess I’m just not man enough
to make up my mind and stick to it.

No doll, I'm not drunk, in fact guess I'm too darn sober—just feel real sentimentily low that's all.
Lots of times you can write things you could never say and I wish I could just sit here and pour out to you
all the things that give me no solution!

I realize that this is a hell of way to start a letter off and you probably wonder what it’s all about—but
then again I think you know.

We left for Vancouver July 13 and got there Thursday afternoon, the 15th. It was wonderful seeing the entire
family again, seeing that most of them are well and the same as ever. The ride up was a little exhausting for Dora
and Laurry because of the heat going thru Fresno, Sacramento, etc. so Eddie decided that they should fly back and
he came back with me alone. We got back last Wednesday evening and now I’m back at work again.

Outwardly I had a wonderful time but after 10 days of being subjected to a word here and there—and a hint here and
there I don’t know where I am—and am mentally so confused—.

Anne I love you so very, very much—your not being here has been a big void in my life. I want you by me and I need
you with me, but I also love my parents and going back there and seeing how they are and how they live and feel about
things brought the nail right to the point for me.

You see doll, having been away from home and that kind of influence for so long I have forgotten, probably because I've
wanted to, I’ve forgotten the clannishsness, the taboos, the must and must—nots of all the people that surrounded my
upbringing.  So when I go back and see that, it hits me right between the eyes harder than ever and I have to ask myself
“Do I dare do this to them?” Then I start thinking of the way __your own__ mother thinks of me and so many like her that
I half-way believe that there may be some reason behind all this enforced segregation—that it would be a horrible mistake
and so many people would get hurt by it.  But then my mind goes back to how much you've meant to me and how very much I
love you, and I’m right back where I started! And you, my poor darling, are right in the centre of the mess.

Oh, if I __only was__ what you once accused me of being, so self-sufficient, how happy I would be!  But you picked
a mouse not a man for a prospective mate, for I just can't close my eyes blindly to conditions and to people who
have had their own style of hopes for me, go ahead and say "To hell with them—come what may!"

Darling, I’m not writing this to you for sympathy, I don’t want to be gushy or corny—but I’ve always been frank with
you and I know you’ll understand my plight.

I’m just going to have to think this thing out that’s all—but the minute I started thinking, emotion sets in and I’m
lost again—! But I just had to get this off my chest to you.

Thank you my sweet for the letter.  I shall answer it when I'm in a different and better mood.

Well here it is 7:15 and Eddie is getting up next door for work—and here an I not having gone to sleep yet,
so will say good-bye for now, my lover.

Please take care of yourself for you're my beloved.

— N
